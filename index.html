00LOD: Licence to Link

How did James Bond become a cultural icon?

A Linked Open Data project exploring the construction of James Bond's cultural identity through literature, cinema, music, objects, recurring motifs and popular culture.

Project Brief

The Project

00LOD: Licence to Link investigates how James Bond developed from Ian Fleming's literary character into a cultural icon recognised across different media and cultural contexts.

The project connects a selected set of entities from literature, cinema, music, material culture and popular culture through Linked Open Data principles.

Research Question How did James Bond become a cultural icon?
