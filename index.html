00LOD: Licence to Link

How did James Bond become a cultural icon?

A Linked Open Data project exploring the construction of James Bond's cultural identity across literature, cinema, music, material culture, recurring motifs and popular culture.

01 / Project Overview

The Project

00LOD: Licence to Link starts from the English Wikipedia article on James Bond and investigates how Ian Fleming's fictional character developed into a cultural icon recognised across different media and cultural contexts.

Fourteen entities were selected to represent Bond's origins, institutional and character systems, material and audiovisual identity, verbal identity and cultural afterlife. Their relations form the basis of the project's knowledge organization and representation workflow.

Research Question How did James Bond become a cultural icon?

02 / Idea Conceptualisation

Mapping the Idea

The project began by mapping the people, works, objects, recurring motifs and cultural events through which James Bond's transformation into a cultural icon could be investigated.
